# Research — 2026-08-08

## YouTube Shorts trends (2026)
- Winning formats cluster around **fast comprehension, strong retention, rewatch
  loops, and instantly-recognizable formats**.
- **Comedy** has the highest virality potential (one good joke = millions).
- **Relatable** ("this is literally me" — gym, office, overthinking) spreads fastest.
- **Transformation** / glow-up / before→after continues to dominate.
- Proven format shells: **Before vs After, Quick Tips, POV Skits, Myth Busting,
  Ranking, Trending Challenge Variations.**
- AI + real-life scenarios is a fast-growing niche; gaming & food pull hundreds of M.
