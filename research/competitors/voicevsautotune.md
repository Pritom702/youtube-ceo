# Competitor teardown — @VoiceVsAutotune (10 top shorts studied 2026-08-08)

Direct competitor for Channel 3 (Real Voice or Autotune). Same lane, massive scale.

## Scale
Top views: **36M, 24M, 13M, 7.5M, 7.3M, 4M, 3.4M, 2.7M, 2.5M, 2.1M, 2M...**
Our Real Voice or Autotune: 1.8–6K. Same idea, ~5000x the result.

## THE FORMULA (consistent across all 10)
1. **Bright SOLID YELLOW background.** Every video. High-contrast, thumb-stopping,
   instantly recognizable brand. The singer performs against the yellow.
2. **On-screen title (top):** "**No Autotune** VS **Autotune**" — "No Autotune" TEAL,
   "VS" white, "Autotune" RED. Identical every video.
3. **YouTube title field:** "Who Sings Better? No Autotune VS Autotune" — a question
   hook that drives comments/debate.
4. **Ranking rail 1–5** down the left (1 green, 2 blue, 3 orange, 4 purple, 5 red).
   Fills in with **artist name + score**: "Oliver Tree 10/10" (green), "Sub Urban
   2/10", "Benson Boone 3/10", "Doja Cat 6/10", "Alec Benjamin 1/10", "Jaleel 4/10".
   Score green (good) / red (bad) = the payoff + debate fuel.
5. **Often ONE artist per video** (the rail shows the same name throughout), ranking
   their vocal across moments — same "one subject" pattern as PepeRanker.
6. **Recognizable artists** (Oliver Tree, Benson Boone, Doja Cat, Alec Benjamin) =
   search intent + shareability + comment wars.
7. **Tight ~15–30s.** Faint center watermark.

## Why they do 36M and our channel does 1.8–6K
| VoiceVsAutotune | Our Real Voice or Autotune |
|---|---|
| Bold consistent YELLOW brand bg | Plain / inconsistent |
| Ranking rail + SCORES (gamified payoff, debate) | A/B only, no scores/gamification |
| Question title "Who Sings Better?" (comment bait) | "Real Voice vs Autotune (5 artists)" |
| Often ONE artist, tight ~15–30s | 5 songs crammed into ~56s |
| Two-tone recognizable title lockup | Weaker lockup |

## Actions for Channel 3
1. **Adopt a bold, consistent branded background** (own a color — pick one, keep it).
2. **Add the ranking rail + numeric SCORES** (green/red) — the score is the payoff and
   drives comment debate.
3. **Question title** ("Who Sings Better?") in the metadata for comment bait.
4. **Tighten to ~15–30s**; consider ONE artist per video (deeper, cleaner).
5. **Lead with recognizable/legendary artists** (our data: Freddie/Whitney overperform).
