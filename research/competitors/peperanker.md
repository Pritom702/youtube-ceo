# Competitor teardown — @PepeRanker (11 top shorts studied 2026-08-08)

Direct competitor in the ranking lane, at massive scale. **This is the model to copy.**

## Scale
Top views: **139M, 41M, 38M, 26M, 22M, 21M, 20M, 20M, 18M, 17M, 16M, 15M...**
Median in the low millions. Ranked Chaos is at 1–3K. Same format family, 1000x the result.

## THE FORMULA (consistent across all 11)
Videos studied: Cologne Atomizers (139M), Chicken Scream (41M/26M/15M), Toilet Design
(38M), Water Fail (22M), Photos BTS (21M), Extreme Wind (20M), Water Ballon (20M),
How Is This Possible (18M), Perfect Fit (17M), Muscle Cramp (16M).

1. **ONE clip, not a compilation.** Each video is a SINGLE oddly-specific viral clip
   (one guy screaming, one rotisserie-toilet contraption, one leg cramping, one hand
   spraying cologne, one POV water slide) — NOT 6 scraped clips. A fake "ranking" rail
   is overlaid on that one continuous clip. **This is the core insight and the opposite
   of what our pipeline does.**
2. **Fake ranking overlay = retention device.** A 1–6 rail down the left, numbers
   colored (1 gold, 2 orange, 3 red, 4–6 white). As the single clip plays, entries
   fill in with SHORT witty scene-specific labels + emoji ("Fast shave 😎", "Level 1
   💀", "Direct hit", "He furious 😡", "Safe spot 😎", "The worst 😖", "Neck bend").
   Best/climax held for last. The labels ARE the comedy.
3. **Title template (burned on top, two lines, centered):**
   **"Ranking [ADJ] [TOPIC] Moments"** — "Ranking" white, ADJ **RED** (Funniest/
   Craziest/Best/Hilarious), TOPIC **YELLOW** (Chicken Scream, Toilet Design...),
   "Moments" white. Identical brand template every video.
4. **YouTube title field = "DON'T CHECK THE SOUND 😭😭"** on nearly every upload — a
   curiosity/unmute hook that drives sound-on + comments. NOT a mistake (corrects our
   earlier lesson). Two-title system: metadata bait + on-screen topic.
5. **Oddly-specific / absurd / satisfying niches** — Chicken Scream, Toilet Design,
   Muscle Cramp, Cologne Atomizers, Extreme Wind, Perfect Fit. Weird = curiosity =
   click. NOT generic "funny fails".
6. **Tight ~15–30s.** Faint "PEPERANKER" watermark center-bottom. Running gag: a bird
   on a bare tree ("chicken") reused as a brand motif.

## Why he does 139M and Ranked Chaos does 1–3K
| PepeRanker | Our Ranked Chaos |
|---|---|
| ONE coherent clip + fake ranking overlay | 6 unrelated scraped clips stitched |
| Scene-specific witty labels (the comedy) | Generic fallback labels ("Nailed it") |
| Oddly-specific curiosity niches | Generic "funny fails" grab-bag |
| Consistent burned title template | Inconsistent |
| "DON'T CHECK THE SOUND" metadata hook | Copied the title but not the format |
| ~15–30s | 45–60s |

## Actions
1. **Rebuild the format: ONE clip + fake ranking overlay**, not 6-clip compilations.
2. **Scene-specific witty labels** with emoji — never generic fallbacks.
3. **Burned title template** "Ranking [ADJ-red] [TOPIC-yellow] Moments".
4. **Two-title system**: metadata "DON'T CHECK THE SOUND 😭😭" + on-screen topic.
5. **Hunt oddly-specific/absurd niches**, tight 15–30s, best held for last.
